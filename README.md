### Getting Started

* [FIFA](https://www.fifa.com/) - The official global football website for World Cup information, international competitions, rankings, news, videos, and football history. ([FIFA][1])
* [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/) - The best official source for European football, including the Champions League, Europa League, national teams, fixtures, results, and rankings. ([UEFA.com][2])
* [Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/) - The official English Premier League website for fixtures, results, tables, clubs, players, statistics, and news.
* [Football Australia](https://www.footballaustralia.com.au/) - The official Australian football organisation covering the Socceroos, Matildas, domestic competitions, and grassroots football.
* [ESPN Soccer](https://www.espn.com/soccer/) - A broad football news and results resource covering major leagues, tournaments, teams, and players.

### Advanced Tools

* [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/) - Excellent for live scores, detailed match statistics, lineups, transfers, news, TV schedules, and player data. ([FotMob][3])
* [Transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.com/) - A comprehensive football database useful for player profiles, transfers, squad values, contracts, competitions, and historical records.
* [FBref](https://fbref.com/) - A powerful statistics database for analysing players, teams, leagues, advanced performance metrics, and historical football data.
* [Understat](https://understat.com/) - A specialised analytics site for exploring expected goals (xG), shot quality, and attacking performance in major European leagues.
* [Opta](https://www.statsperform.com/opta/) - A leading professional football data provider offering detailed event data and advanced performance analytics used across the football industry.
